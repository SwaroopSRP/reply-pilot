# Billing Policy

## Duplicate Charges
If a customer is charged more than once for the same order or billing cycle, support must verify the payment records in the database before recommending or issuing a refund.
Confirmed duplicate transactions for the same order or billing period are fully eligible for a prompt refund back to the original payment method.

## Failed Payments and Retries
When a recurring payment fails, our billing processor automatically retries the payment up to 3 times over 7 days.
During this grace period, the customer's account and access remain active.
If payment fails on the final attempt, the subscription status transitions to `past_due` and account features are restricted until updated billing information is provided.

## Billing Cycle and Renewal Notices
Monthly and annual plans renew automatically at the start of each billing period unless cancelled prior to the renewal date.
Annual subscribers receive an automated email notice 14 days prior to their renewal date detailing the upcoming renewal amount.

## Invoice and Tax Documentation
Customers can download VAT/sales tax invoices directly from their Account Settings under the Billing tab.
Support agents may resend PDF receipts for any completed transaction upon customer request.
