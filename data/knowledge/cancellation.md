# Cancellation Policy

## Subscription Cancellation Procedure
Customers may cancel their subscription at any time directly through their Account Settings page under "Subscription > Cancel Plan", or by contacting support.
When a cancellation request is received, support should confirm the customer's identity and guide them through the self-service steps or process it on their behalf.

## Effective Cancellation Date
Cancellations take effect at the conclusion of the customer's current paid billing cycle.
Customers retain full access to their plan features and historical data until the expiration date of their prepaid period.
We do not issue automatic prorated refunds for remaining days within an active monthly billing period unless explicitly required under the 14-day refund window.

## Account Pause Option
Customers seeking to temporarily halt their subscription due to travel, project lulls, or budget constraints can pause their account for up to 90 days.
During a pause, all data and configurations are preserved, and no recurring charges occur. Access resumes automatically at the end of the selected pause duration.

## Data Retention Post-Cancellation
Customer data is preserved in read-only state for 60 days following the end of the final billing cycle.
After 60 days, inactive project files and database records are scheduled for permanent deletion in compliance with data privacy regulations.
