# Escalation Policy

## Tier 1 Support Scope
Tier 1 support handles general product inquiries, standard account navigation, routine password resets, basic billing questions, and standard 14-day refunds.
All standard responses should follow our grounded tone and policy guidelines.

## Mandatory Escalation Triggers (Human Review)
Support requests must be marked as requiring human review and escalated to Tier 2 or Senior Operations under the following circumstances:
- High priority cases involving payments or billing disputes greater than $100
- Confirmed delayed shipments exceeding 3 business days past the promised estimated delivery date
- Chargeback notifications or legal threats received from a customer
- Requests involving security incidents, compromised accounts, or unauthorized plan changes
- Requests with ambiguous or contradictory information where policy cannot be deterministically applied
- Customer messages exhibiting high frustration, profanity, or threatening churn of Enterprise accounts

## Shipping Delays and Carrier Investigations
If an order's tracking shows no movement for more than 48 hours or is 3+ days overdue past ETA:
1. Verify the shipping address and carrier tracking number in the orders database.
2. Open a carrier investigation ticket with our logistics liaison.
3. Issue a courtesy status update to the customer with the carrier reference number and offer expedited replacement if the package is declared lost by day 7.
