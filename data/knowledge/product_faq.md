# Product FAQ

## Subscription Plans and Pricing
- Starter Plan: $19/month ($190/year billed annually). Includes up to 3 team members, 10 GB storage, and standard email support (response within 24 hours).
- Pro Plan: $49/month ($490/year billed annually). Includes up to 10 team members, 100 GB storage, priority email & chat support (response within 4 hours), and advanced integrations.
- Enterprise Plan: $199/month ($1,990/year billed annually). Includes unlimited team members, 1 TB storage, dedicated account manager, phone support, custom SSO/SAML, and 99.9% uptime SLA.

## Plan Upgrades and Downgrades
Upgrades take effect immediately. The account is charged a prorated amount for the remaining duration of the current billing cycle.
Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle. The customer continues to have access to higher-tier features until the end of the current paid period.

## Physical Hardware & Accessories
Hardware orders (such as dedicated security keys and hub accessories) ship via standard delivery (3 to 5 business days) or express delivery (1 to 2 business days).
Tracking numbers are issued via email within 24 hours of fulfillment.
All hardware accessories carry a 1-year limited warranty covering manufacturer defects.
