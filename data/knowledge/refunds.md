# Refund Policy

## 14-Day Refund Guarantee
Customers are eligible for a full refund on any new subscription or renewal if the refund request is submitted within 14 calendar days of the charge date.
No questions are required if requested within this 14-day window.

## Outside 14-Day Policy Window
Requests received after 14 calendar days from the charge date are generally non-refundable.
In exceptional circumstances (such as documented prolonged service outages or severe technical impediments caused by our platform), a Tier 2 team lead or manager may authorize a prorated credit or partial refund.

## Processing Timelines and Methods
All approved refunds must be issued directly to the original payment method used for the purchase.
Refunds cannot be routed to alternative credit cards, third parties, or bank accounts.
Once initiated, credit card and digital wallet refunds typically appear on the customer's statement within 3 to 5 business days, depending on their financial institution.

## Non-Refundable Items
The following items and charges are strictly non-refundable:
- One-time setup, onboarding, or custom implementation fees
- Hardware or peripheral items after 30 days or if returned with visible physical damage
- Accounts terminated for verified abuse or violations of our Terms of Service
