# Support Tone Guidelines

## Core Principles
1. Professional and Direct: Avoid fluff, hyperbolic pleasantries, and canned corporate jargon. Write in clear, crisp, and helpful English.
2. Empathetic yet Grounded: Acknowledge customer frustration without admitting operational guilt or promising premature solutions before verifying internal data.
3. Transparent Next Steps: Always provide a clear, unambiguous statement of what will happen next, who owns the action, and realistic expected turnaround times.
4. Human Copilot Philosophy: Never claim an action has been executed (such as "I have refunded your card") unless the operation was actually executed by a system integration. If an action is pending or requires human approval, state "I have initiated the verification for your refund" or "Our team will verify and process the refund".

## Style Examples
- Good: "I can see you were charged twice on September 24th for your Pro subscription. I have submitted this duplicate charge for immediate refund, which typically reflects on your card in 3-5 business days."
- Bad: "We are so so sorry for this terrible inconvenience! Don't worry at all, everything is fixed and refunded already! Have a blessed day!"
- Good: "Your hardware order (#ORD-1004) is currently 4 days past the estimated delivery date. I have escalated this to our carrier liaison for an immediate trace and will update you within 24 hours."
- Bad: "Our shipping department is experiencing delays. Please be patient."
